## Video

Embeds a YouTube video on the board, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (`youtube-nocookie.com`).

### Configuration

- **Title / Subtitle** — optional labels shown in the gadget header.
- **YouTube URL or Video ID** — accepts a full YouTube URL (`youtube.com/watch?v=...`, `youtu.be/...`, `youtube.com/shorts/...`) or a bare 11-character video ID.
- **Autoplay (muted)** — starts playback automatically when the board loads. Browsers only allow autoplay when the video is muted, so this also mutes it; viewers can unmute from the player controls.

### Example

```
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
```
